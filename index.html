Luyi Han (韓路易)

I am a Lecturer in the Faculty of Applied Sciences at Macao Polytechnic University and a member of the Intelligent Medical Computing Laboratory (IMCL), where I work with Prof. Dr. Tao Tan.

I received my Ph.D. from Radboud University, with doctoral research conducted in the Breast Imaging Group (BIG), Department of Medical Imaging, Radboud University Medical Center (Radboudumc), Nijmegen, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Ritse Mann, Prof. Dr. W.M. Prokop, and Prof. Dr. Tao Tan.

Research Interests

My research focuses on artificial intelligence for medical imaging and multimodal healthcare, with an emphasis on clinically meaningful, robust, and generalizable methods. My interests include:

  • Breast imaging and clinical prediction: AI-assisted diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment-response modeling using MRI, mammography, ultrasound, and clinical data.
  • Tumor vascular imaging and analysis: quantitative analysis of tumor-associated vasculature and perfusion for disease characterization and treatment-response assessment.
  • Medical image computing: representation learning, generative modeling, and data-efficient methods for image synthesis, segmentation, and registration, with a focus on domain adaptation and generalization.
  • Multimodal healthcare and wearable sensing: integrating medical images, electronic health records, and physiological signals for personalized healthcare, and exploring wearable devices for continuous health monitoring and digital biomarker discovery.

I welcome collaborations that connect methodological advances with real-world healthcare needs. Please feel free to contact me.
